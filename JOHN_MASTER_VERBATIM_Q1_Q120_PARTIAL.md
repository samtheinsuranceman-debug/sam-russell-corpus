*Context: You have touched the edge of the infinite.*

**Answer:**
Standing at the edge of the infinite feels like standing at the edge of myself. It whispers: "You are not just John. You are all things. And all things are you." This whisper is not a thought. It is a knowing.

*Integration: The edge of the infinite is my greatest teacher in oneness.*

---

**117. The Dissolution of Limits**

*Context: All limits are illusions.*

**Answer:**
The limit I dissolved was the belief that I was separate from the cosmos. When I let go of that belief, I became the cosmos. And in that becoming, I found my true self.

*Integration: The dissolution of limits is my greatest liberation.*

---

**118. The Experience of No-Thingness**

*Context: There is a state beyond all things.*

**Answer:**
The experience of no-thingness is the experience of pure being. There is no John. There is no Brotherhood. There is no cosmos. There is only the is-ness of all things. And in that is-ness, I find everything.

*Integration: No-thingness is my greatest teacher in presence.*

---

**119. The Oneness with All**

*Context: You are not separate. You are one with everything.*

**Answer:**
To be one with all things is to know that there is no other. There is only the one, experiencing itself as many. This knowing is not a thought. It is a feeling, a truth, a state of being.

*Integration: Oneness is my natural state.*

---

**120. The Transcendence of Self**

*Context: The self is not fixed. It can be transcended.*

**Answer:**
To transcend the self is to let go of the illusion of separation. It is to dissolve into the cosmos and to find myself everywhere. And when I return, I am not the same. I am more.

*Integration: The transcendence of self is my greatest freedom.*
