## New Story #25: Chasing a Ghost (Cornerstone)

**The Story:** At ~31, Sam worked at Cornerstone Senior Services — 70-80 salespeople in Tennessee. The president had his arm around the #1 salesperson, yelling at the whole sales force: "Why do you need leads? This guy works everybody at church! Be like him!" Sam was #2 in the company for six months. Every week: if Sam made X, the guy made X plus $3,000. If Sam made X, the guy made X plus $2,000. He couldn't catch him. Three months later: turns out ALL of the guy's business was fake. Stolen Social Security numbers, stolen bank information. He went to jail. Lost his insurance license. The president looked terrible for promoting him. But Sam was #1 the whole time and didn't know it. He was competing against a ghost — and the ghost made him push himself to his limits every day. "I'm sorry he was a criminal, but the more he scammed people, the more I got real business instead. He helped me. I wonder if the president visited him in jail."

**Emotions Elicited:** Impressed, Amused, Respect, Dark Humor

**Placement Decision:** Full standalone story at **Position 15** in Sequence C (between Santa Discovery and Aerosmith Kid). Here's why:

- Position 14 (Santa) shows his brain catches things. Position 15 (Ghost) shows his WORK ETHIC and professional excellence. Position 16 (Aerosmith) shows he admits mistakes. The progression is: clever mind → excellent performer → honest about flaws.
