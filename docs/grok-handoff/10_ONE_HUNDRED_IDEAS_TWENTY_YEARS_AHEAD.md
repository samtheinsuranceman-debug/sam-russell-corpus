# One hundred ideas, fifteen to twenty years ahead (2026-09-06)

The question: what would the best financial website that could ever exist look
like for physicians and practice owners, and what has to happen in the world for
it to arrive?

This is a panel answer, not one model's. Five outside voices were asked the same
brief in parallel and their proposals were merged, de-duplicated, and ranked here:

| Voice | Contribution |
|---|---|
| GPT-5 | identity wallet, zero-knowledge compliance, transaction firewall, health-adjusted twin, data union, programmable trusts, money hub |
| Gemini 2.5 Pro | immutable advice log, cognitive-decline protocol, dynamic valuation, liability hedging, domicile planner, regulator API, ethicist console |
| Grok 4.3 | claims arbitrator, family veto ledger, tail hedge engine, entity migration agent, stress-test marketplace, successor hand-off, cap-table rebalancer |
| Mistral Large | family constitution, plan-B engine, future-income loans, time banking, self-healing reconciliation, librarian with memory |
| Perplexity (web-grounded) | the evidence for what is already moving: agentic-AI supervision (FINRA 2026), CFPB 1033 data rights, verifiable credentials, GENIUS Act stablecoins, atomic settlement, FHIR health data, IRS IRIS, SECURE 2.0, climate insurability, Reg S-P |
| Claude | synthesis, ranking, and the preconditions |

## Part 1: what has to happen for this future to arrive

Eight preconditions. Every one of the hundred ideas below rests on at least one.

1. **Consumer-directed data rights become real.** Bank, brokerage, tax and
   practice data flow to whoever the client authorizes, revocably, with
   provenance. The CFPB 1033 rule is the first draft of this; the platform is
   built so it works whether the pipe is a direct bank API or a regulated
   aggregator.
2. **AI agents get bounded legal authority.** A client can grant an agent a
   scoped mandate (this account, this ceiling, this purpose, this window) that
   regulators recognise, with human gates, audit and rollback. FINRA's 2026
   guidance on agent autonomy is the opening move.
3. **Identity becomes cryptographic.** Licences, board certification, DEA, NPI,
   privileges, entity officer roles and family relationships are verifiable
   credentials, not PDFs. Every counterparty checks them in milliseconds.
4. **Money becomes programmable.** Instant settlement, tokenized deposits and
   regulated stablecoins (GENIUS Act) mean a plan can move value the moment a
   verified event happens, with reversal windows.
5. **Law becomes machine-readable.** Tax code, state asset-protection statutes,
   corporate-practice-of-medicine rules and plan documents are versioned rule
   engines, so a plan recomputes the day the law changes.
6. **Health data joins the plan with consent.** FHIR feeds, wearables and
   clinical records inform longevity, disability and care planning, and are
   walled off from underwriting unless the client opts in.
7. **Trust is proven, not promised.** Every recommendation carries a signed,
   explainable justification; compliance can be proven without exposing private
   data; advisors, carriers and lenders are scored on ledgered outcomes.
8. **The advisor's job changes.** From calculator to overseer: exceptions,
   ethics, judgment, and the human conversation. The platform runs the plan; the
   advisor is accountable for it.

## Part 2: the hundred, ranked

Rank is by how much depends on it. Foundations first, then data, engines,
practice ownership, family and legacy, then money and markets. Each entry:
what it is, why it changes the game, and what must be true.

### Foundations of trust and authority (1–15)

1. **Consent Ledger.** Granular, time-boxed, revocable permissions for every
   data source and every person (spouse, CPA, attorney, heirs, carriers), recorded
   on the Plan Ledger. Deep automation becomes safe because every read and write
   is authorised and auditable. Needs: precondition 1.
2. **Fiduciary Transaction Firewall.** A policy engine plus an AI co-signer that
   approves, delays or blocks any money movement against plan rules, risk limits
   and conflicts of interest. Errors and bad actors are stopped before funds move,
   and advisor liability becomes provably controlled. Needs: 2, 4.
3. **Signed Advice Log.** Every recommendation, the data it used, the assumptions
   and the rules it satisfied, signed and appended to the ledger in plain language.
   The AI's reasoning is transparent, legally defensible and readable by the client.
   Needs: 7.
4. **Zero-Knowledge Compliance Proofs.** Cryptographic proof that a
   recommendation met fiduciary and suitability rules without revealing the
   client's data. Audits shrink from months to seconds and regulators can verify
   in real time. Needs: 7.
5. **Scoped Agent Mandates.** The client grants each agent bounded authority
   (account, ceiling, purpose, duration) with approval gates and one-click
   rollback. Agents can act, and nobody has to trust them blindly. Needs: 2.
6. **Physician Identity Wallet.** Self-sovereign verifiable credentials for
   licence, certs, DEA, NPI, privileges and entity roles that sign carrier,
   lender and payer agreements. Onboarding collapses from weeks to minutes and
   pricing improves because risk is source-verified. Needs: 3.
7. **Licence and KYC Harmoniser.** Credentials and KYC are maintained once and
   propagated to every counterparty. Any account, policy or loan opens immediately
   with no repeated paperwork. Needs: 3.
8. **Portable Plan Bundle.** The whole decision history, data lineage and rule
   set export as a machine-readable package. A successor advisor, or a different
   platform, reconstructs the plan without a narrative handover. Needs: 1, 7.
9. **Regulator Window.** An anonymised, real-time compliance feed regulators can
   subscribe to, plus a sandbox where proposed rules are tested against real plan
   shapes. Trust is earned upstream and restrictive rules are shaped together.
   Needs: 7.
10. **Deepfake-Proof Money Movement.** Phishing-resistant MFA, device and
    session risk scoring, out-of-band confirmation, cooling-off periods and
    trusted-contact escalation on every transfer. Voice clones and synthetic
    identities stop being a way in. Needs: 3.
11. **Data-Trust Escrow.** Aggregation feeds and tax transcripts sit in an escrow
    that releases only to approved engines, or to a regulator under order.
    Aggregation expands without creating a single point of failure. Needs: 1.
12. **Cognitive Capacity Safeguards.** A protocol the client sets while sharp:
    objective capacity checks that step control from alerts, to spending caps,
    to co-signers, to delegation. Independence is preserved early and protected
    late, with no family fight. Needs: 2, 6.
13. **Event-Driven Plan Runtime.** The plan reacts to verified events (cash
    received, claim approved, threshold crossed, beneficiary change requested)
    with authorised, idempotent, reversible actions. The plan runs itself between
    meetings. Needs: 2, 4.
14. **Advisor Ethicist Console.** The human sees the recommendations the AI
    flagged as optimal-but-questionable (a high-risk path for a risk-averse
    client) and rules on them. The advisor's role becomes judgment, which no model
    replaces. Needs: 8.
15. **Provenance Document Vault.** Every document signed, timestamped and
    lineage-tracked; every will, trust or buy-sell amendment checked against the
    active plan at drafting time. Conflicts surface before probate, never after.
    Needs: 7.

### Data and gathering (16–35)

16. **Consented Health-Financial Bridge.** Claims, coverage and
    explanation-of-benefits data flow in through FHIR with granular scopes, kept
    apart from underwriting. Care costs and coverage gaps become plan inputs
    instead of surprises. Needs: 6.
17. **Health-Adjusted Financial Twin.** Wearables, records and shift load
    forecast disability, burnout and recovery windows. Insurance, savings and
    workload are tuned to the physician's real trajectory, not the population
    average. Needs: 6.
18. **Longevity Probability Bands.** Individual, updating life-expectancy
    distributions replace static tables and drive adaptive drawdown. Neither
    premature depletion nor needless austerity. Needs: 6.
19. **Lifetime Data Timeline.** Training, credential renewals, call schedules,
    case mix, income streams and family events on one causal timeline. Planners
    see cause and effect and pre-empt stress points. Needs: 1.
20. **Practice Revenue Autopilot Rail.** Direct pipes to payers and
    clearinghouses forecast reimbursements, denials and remittance timing, then
    pre-route cash to taxes, payroll, debt and reserves. Liquidity stops being
    reactive. Needs: 1, 4.
21. **Autonomous Practice CFO.** Reconciles remittances to fee schedules, flags
    underpayments, files appeals, forecasts receivables by line of business. Lost
    revenue is recovered and planning inputs become reliable. Needs: 2.
22. **Payer-Contract Simulator.** Net present value of a proposed panel contract
    from the physician's own procedure volume and denial history. Negotiation
    moves from gut feel to quantified trade-offs before signature. Needs: 1.
23. **Value-Based-Care Panel.** Attribution, quality incentives, capitation and
    downside risk connected to owner distributions and valuation. Personal plans
    stop ignoring the contracts that drive the practice. Needs: 1, 6.
24. **Procedure-Code Risk Forecaster.** Future CPT valuation changes modelled
    against billing patterns to size disability and life cover to real revenue
    volatility. Income protection fits the person, not the specialty average.
    Needs: 1.
25. **Real-Time Tax Fact Feed.** Machine-readable tax data in (IRS IRIS-style
    APIs) and estimated payments out, under explicit authorisation and audit.
    Tax stops being a once-a-year reconstruction. Needs: 1, 5.
26. **Versioned Tax-Rules Engine.** Estimated taxes, withholding, entity
    compensation, Roth-versus-pre-tax and equipment timing recompute the day
    legislation changes. The next tax act is absorbed in hours. Needs: 5.
27. **Household Direct-Indexing Engine.** Security-level lots, wash-sale
    detection across spouses and trusts, gains budgets, concentrated-stock
    handling. Tax-personalised portfolios at scale. Needs: 1.
28. **Continuous Harvesting Agent.** Bounded by a mandate, harvests losses and
    pairs gains across every account daily, not yearly. Transient volatility
    becomes tax alpha. Needs: 2.
29. **Sibling-Practice Benchmark Pool.** De-identified peer data on overhead,
    payer mix and growth. Owners learn whether their numbers are outliers or
    norms. Needs: 1.
30. **Physician Data Union.** Members pool de-identified income, claims and
    coding data under terms they set, and the licence income comes back to them.
    Better-priced products and a new income stream. Needs: 1, 3.
31. **Location Insurability Score.** Catastrophe and climate scoring for home,
    clinic and surgery centre, with premiums, exclusions, backup sites and
    uninsured-loss scenarios. Insurability is now a planning variable. Needs: 1.
32. **Digital-Estate Inventory.** Wallets, domains, online businesses, account
    credentials and key-location instructions, kept out of the will and the
    ordinary database. Digital assets stop dying with their owner. Needs: 3, 7.
33. **Aggregation Mode Switch.** Direct bank APIs or regulated aggregators,
    swapped as the data-rights rule evolves, with provenance on every field.
    The platform never depends on one pipe. Needs: 1.
34. **Self-Healing Reconciliation.** The Plan Ledger reconciles continuously to
    bank, tax and practice data, flagging and correcting discrepancies. Monthly
    chores become a live, trusted state. Needs: 1.
35. **Librarian with Memory.** The advisor AI recalls every interaction across
    years and surfaces it when it matters ("last time you considered selling, the
    concern was X; here is what changed"). Advice becomes cumulative. Needs: 7.

### Engines 2.0, physician-specific (36–55)

36. **Malpractice Tail Hedge Engine.** Reprices tail-coverage needs continuously
    from procedure mix, emerging case law and carrier solvency. Coverage
    decisions stop being renewal-date events. Needs: 5.
37. **Real-Time Liability Hedging.** Quantifies lawsuit exposure by specialty,
    location and volume, and structures a liquid counterweight portfolio. Beyond
    insurance: a financial hedge built from the founder's own field. Needs: 1.
38. **Parametric Malpractice Cover.** Payouts trigger on verified events
    (docket filings, coded adverse events) at pre-agreed amounts. Claims resolve
    fast and personal finances are insulated from tail risk. Needs: 3, 4.
39. **Premium Optimiser.** Risk-retention groups, captives and high-deductible
    structures tested quarterly against actual loss history. Premium spend
    becomes a managed variable. Needs: 1.
40. **Reputation-Based Underwriting.** Opt-in outcomes, peer review and
    continuing-education records earn lower premiums, with the reasoning shown.
    Excellence gets a price. Needs: 3, 6.
41. **Modular Insurance Builder.** Life, disability, long-term-care and annuity
    riders assembled across carriers to the verified profile and health twin.
    Coverage fits precisely and updates as life changes. Needs: 3, 6.
42. **Pre-Underwriting Packager.** With consent, a carrier-ready file with an
    explainable risk narrative from records, labs and occupational exposure.
    Underwriting becomes instant and cheaper. Needs: 6.
43. **Embedded Insurance at the Point of Need.** Quote, bind and renew inside the
    planning journey the moment a gap is detected. Protection stops being a
    separate annual errand. Needs: 3.
44. **Locums Income Balancer.** Watches schedules and cash flow, bids for the
    right shifts with bundled malpractice and credential verification. Income
    gaps close without burnout. Needs: 3.
45. **Cross-Border Telemedicine Payroll.** Licensure checks, withholding,
    treaties and FX hedging on earnings across states and countries. Global work
    becomes safe. Needs: 3, 5.
46. **Clinical Side-Income Router.** Speaking, trials, consulting and
    moonlighting routed through compliant entities with conflict logs and tax
    allocation. Side income helps the plan without regulatory missteps. Needs: 5.
47. **Career Trajectory Simulator.** Lifetime earnings across employed, private,
    academic and sub-specialty paths from regional data. Early choices are made
    with the long picture visible. Needs: 1.
48. **Residency-to-Retirement Continuity.** Training loans, first contracts and
    partnership tracks in one unbroken plan file. Early decisions are judged by
    their effect on later exits. Needs: 1.
49. **Retirement-Plan Automation.** Employer-plan data, catch-up eligibility,
    Roth rules and student-loan matches integrated per household and practice.
    SECURE 2.0 features stop being paperwork. Needs: 1, 5.
50. **Dynamic Domicile Planner.** Travel, asset location and income sources
    analysed against state and federal rules, with legal guardrails. Residency
    becomes a strategic, lawful decision. Needs: 5.
51. **Entity Migration Agent.** When rules change, drafts the minimal
    conversions and retitlings, for attorney review. Entity structure becomes a
    live variable instead of a five-year commitment. Needs: 5.
52. **Case-Law-Linked Asset Protection.** Trust situs, exemptions and titling
    rules update as statutes and rulings shift across states. Protection stays
    current without constant legal rework. Needs: 5.
53. **Asset-Protection Monitor.** Watches titling, coverage and structures, and
    proposes moves the moment exposure spikes; nothing moves without approval.
    Protection becomes continuous. Needs: 2, 5.
54. **Personal Black-Swan Simulator.** Career-ending injury, licence loss,
    disruptive technology, and their clustering. Resilience against the disasters
    that actually happen to physicians. Needs: 1.
55. **Multi-Decade Cash-Flow Oracle.** After-tax spendable income to age 95 under
    thousands of reimbursement and volume paths. Spending rules follow the real
    trajectory, not a generic withdrawal rate. Needs: 1, 6.

### Practice ownership and exit (56–72)

56. **Dynamic Practice Valuation.** Daily value from financials, goodwill,
    patient churn, local demographics and data value. Owners watch their largest
    asset the way they watch a stock. Needs: 1.
57. **Live Physician Cap Table.** Stakes, votes, liquidation preferences and
    waterfall distributions with dispute rules. Handshake deals become
    transparent, enforceable structure. Needs: 3.
58. **Cap-Table Rebalancer.** Partner exits and entries recompute buy-sell terms
    and funding from live cash flow. Transitions lose their negotiation friction.
    Needs: 1, 3.
59. **Practice Exit Navigator.** Private-equity, MSO, ESOP, partner buy-in and
    hospital scenarios with payer-mix forecasts and staff-retention costs, then
    LOIs and diligence rooms. Owners close faster with fewer surprises. Needs: 1.
60. **PC/MSO Structure Modeller.** Rollover equity, earn-outs, covenants,
    minority ownership and state corporate-practice rules. The practice is
    modelled as what it legally is. Needs: 5.
61. **Succession Marketplace.** Owners set parameters; successors are scanned and
    pre-qualified on financial, professional and philosophical fit. Succession
    runs in the background for years. Needs: 3.
62. **Tokenised Practice Equity.** Fractional, collateralisable ownership under
    securities law. Liquidity for a traditionally illiquid asset without a full
    sale. Needs: 4.
63. **Ownership Time Banking.** Junior partners bank future stakes for current
    cash flow under performance-adjusted terms. Succession becomes flexible and
    fair. Needs: 4.
64. **Conditional Capital Calls.** Contingent funding released when verifiable
    milestones are met. Fundraising friction disappears and interests align.
    Needs: 4.
65. **Inter-Physician Loan Marketplace.** Ledger-verified cash flow lets one
    owner lend to another under reviewed terms with repayment from receivables.
    Capital circulates inside the profession. Needs: 3, 4.
66. **Equipment Purchase Escrow.** Payments release on attested uptime and
    usage. Nobody pays for idle or defective equipment. Needs: 4.
67. **Contract and Premium Negotiator.** Negotiates payer contracts, malpractice
    premiums, leases and vendor terms with pooled data. The single advisor gets a
    procurement department. Needs: 1, 2.
68. **Renegotiation Agent.** Monitors market rates for employment, insurance and
    vendor agreements and opens renegotiation when terms improve. Static
    multi-year deals become dynamic. Needs: 2.
69. **Group Practice Console.** Shared practice finances for partners, strictly
    private personal plans. The partnership dilemma solved. Needs: 1.
70. **Post-Retirement Credential Lease.** Consulting and licence rights leased
    with exposure-adjusted, tax-aware terms. Human capital keeps earning after the
    last shift. Needs: 3.
71. **Cash-Flow Oracle with Pre-Approved Actions.** Forecasts from billing,
    payroll and records, then triggers line draws, tax payments and contributions
    the owner pre-authorised. Feast-or-famine ends. Needs: 2, 4.
72. **Autonomous Claims Arbitrator.** Limited authority to dispute denials with
    ledger-backed evidence. Collections become predictable; the advisor only
    reviews exceptions. Needs: 2.

### Family, longevity, legacy (73–88)

73. **Programmable Family Trusts.** Roles, age gates, education milestones,
    medical contingencies and emergency overrides enforced by custodial APIs.
    Intent executes faithfully without constant legal rework. Needs: 4, 5.
74. **Family Financial Constitution.** Values, decision rules and dispute
    mechanisms codified and enforced. Family governance becomes a hard system.
    Needs: 5.
75. **Family Veto Ledger.** Spouses and adult children as read-only or
    conditional-approval nodes on the Plan Ledger above set thresholds. Disputes
    move from litigation to pre-agreed governance. Needs: 3.
76. **Multi-Sig Family Treasury.** Thresholds, time locks and overrides on the
    accounts that fund budgets, education and elder care. Shared control, no
    single point of failure. Needs: 4.
77. **Generational Transfer Agent.** Multi-decade distributions executed on
    verified life events. The grantor's intent outlives the grantor. Needs: 2, 3.
78. **Self-Executing First-Phase Settlement.** An oracle-confirmed death triggers
    the certain, uncontested parts of an estate. Probate's slowest phase
    disappears. Needs: 3, 4.
79. **Estate and Disability Autopilot.** Retitling, notifications and account
    instructions fire on verified registry events. Families see the plan act.
    Needs: 2, 3.
80. **Inter-Generational Asset Exchange.** Compliant family transfers that
    optimise basis and brackets across generations. Informal family support
    becomes a tax-efficient tool. Needs: 5.
81. **Education Funding Automaton.** 529 and trust releases against verified
    enrolment and cost. Goals execute without annual re-authorisation. Needs: 3.
82. **Longevity-Linked Instruments.** Annuity, loan and insurance terms that
    adjust to consented healthspan data. Money aligns with the actual life.
    Needs: 6.
83. **Income Operating System.** Social Security, annuities, pensions,
    withdrawals, brackets, healthcare costs, survivor needs and dynamic spending
    rules in one decumulation engine. Retirement advice becomes income advice.
    Needs: 5.
84. **Care-Cost Planner with Health Triggers.** Long-term-care scenarios,
    product comparisons and reserves that update on health status. The most
    ignored risk becomes a managed one. Needs: 6.
85. **Family Office as a Service.** Household payroll, property and bills run by
    agents inside the plan. Private-office administration at a physician's scale.
    Needs: 2.
86. **Outcome-Linked Philanthropy.** Giving disbursed against verified impact,
    with receipts on the ledger and tax timing optimised. Generosity with
    evidence. Needs: 3.
87. **Family Risk Graph.** Opt-in view across the extended family: care needs,
    estate cliffs, shared practice liabilities. Family planning becomes a living
    stress test. Needs: 1.
88. **Plan-B Engine.** Every major decision carries a parallel contingency plan
    (disability, divorce, sale) with triggers to switch. Contingency planning
    becomes executable. Needs: 2.

### Money movement and markets (89–100)

89. **Programmable Money Hub.** ACH, real-time payments, tokenised deposits,
    escrow or cross-border rails chosen per policy, timing and risk. Cash follows
    plan intent automatically. Needs: 4.
90. **Regulator-Approved Execution Hooks.** Pre-cleared templates: dual
    human-plus-agent signatures, then movement. Faster and more auditable than
    manual approval. Needs: 2, 4.
91. **Atomic Settlement Ledger.** Delivery-versus-payment, 24/7, with tax lots
    updated at the instant of settlement. Settlement risk contained. Needs: 4.
92. **Tokenised Asset Accounting.** Token type, issuer, legal ownership, basis,
    lock-ups, transfer rules and corporate actions recorded without assuming
    every token is a security. The plan understands what it holds. Needs: 4.
93. **Private and Fractional Asset Suitability.** Accreditation, lock-ups,
    capital calls, valuation, K-1s and concentration assessed, not just a price
    displayed. Access with judgment. Needs: 4.
94. **Future-Income Loans.** Borrowing against verified future earnings with
    repayment that adjusts to actual income. Lenders and physicians aligned
    without personal guarantees. Needs: 3, 4.
95. **Regulated Cash Management.** Stablecoins, tokenised deposits, money-market
    funds and insured balances distinguished by yield, reserve, insurance, tax
    and counterparty. Cash choices made with the rules in view. Needs: 4.
96. **Stress-Test Marketplace.** Outside researchers submit macro and medical
    scenarios; the platform prices their effect on each ledger in exchange for
    anonymised access. Forward visibility no single firm could build. Needs: 1.
97. **Execution-Backed Reputation Graph.** Carriers, lenders and vendors scored
    on ledgered outcomes: pricing met, claims paid on time, appeals won. Selection
    by proof, not marketing. Needs: 7.
98. **Russell Number on the Ledger.** The advisor score attested by opted-in
    client outcomes, portable and verifiable. The person guiding you is measured
    against a standard. Needs: 7.
99. **Advisor Orchestration.** Specialists assembled per engagement, with the
    platform as fiduciary orchestrator. The monolithic advisor becomes a
    conductor. Needs: 8.
100. **Bounded Compliance Agents.** Fraud, anti-money-laundering and
    surveillance agents with explainable alerts, protected evidence and human
    disposition of every consequential case. The house stays clean as it scales.
    Needs: 2, 7.

## Part 3: where to start

Items 1, 2, 3, 5, 13 and 15 are buildable now on the existing Plan Ledger and
event bus, and everything else attaches to them. Items 16, 25 and 26 depend on
data pipes that exist today (FHIR, IRIS, aggregators) and consent that the
platform already records. The rest wait on the preconditions in Part 1, and the
platform is built so that when each one lands, the corresponding ideas switch on
rather than get rebuilt.
