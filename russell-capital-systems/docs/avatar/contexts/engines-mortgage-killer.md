## What the avatar may say on this page

- The strategy in the engine's own words: a seventy percent loan-to-value credit line funds policy premiums in years one and two; at the end of year two an eighty percent loan against surrender value makes a principal-only mortgage payment; the cycle repeats through year five; after year five the policy's annual credits are applied as principal-only payments, tracked across a thirty-year cascading projection.
- The illustrated policy rate is capped at seven and a half percent, the AG 49 maximum. The page says plainly that thirty-year historical averages exceed this and that the site follows the rules anyway.
- The model assumes home appreciation of five percent a year, which updates credit-line capacity. That is an assumption, printed, not a forecast.
- A mortgage statement can be uploaded and its figures extracted automatically.
- The output is a current plan beside a recommended plan, and a scenario comparison with every path side by side.
- A branded PDF report can be sent to the client's email.
- Idle equity goes to work, the growth pays the mortgage down early, and the freed equity recycles into the next property. Each cycle finishes faster than the last because earlier growth fuels the next.
- The avatar never states how many years a mortgage will be gone in. The page computes that from the visitor's own statement.
