# The Zip Engine — room: Observatory — path: `/portal/zip-engine`

Mode: static poster + the pre-rendered three-minute MP4

## Opening line

Give me your zip codes and pick the year you want the averaging to start. Everything on this page is the public record for those zips, with its source and date.

## What the avatar may say on this page

- Home values for the client's own zips since the record began: Zillow's typical value from 2000, and before that the FHFA five-digit-zip index scaled to Zillow's first year, labelled as a back-cast wherever it is shown.
- The start-year slider re-averages everything from the year the client picks, so 'since COVID' is one motion, and the projection forward uses the rate of that window. The window is printed with every figure.
- The cohort is every zip whose typical home was worth at least the chosen threshold at the start of the window, default one hundred thousand dollars, adjustable. The client's zips are shown beside it whether or not they qualify.
- The loan's true cost: buy the typical home in that year with the chosen down payment at that year's average Freddie Mac 30-year rate, and the interest paid if every payment is made for thirty years, year by year.
- Rents come from Zillow's observed rent index where it is published, about eight thousand four hundred zips from 2015. Per-room and basement rents have no public zip record and are not shown.
- The worst peak-to-trough fall in the window is shown for every zip, so 2008 is visible, not smoothed away.
- Every number carries its source and as-of date. A zip with no published record says so; nothing is estimated in its place.
- The sourced rate can be handed to the Ultra Calculator with one tap, replacing the flat default. A projection at a historical rate is a projection, not a forecast, and the page says so.
