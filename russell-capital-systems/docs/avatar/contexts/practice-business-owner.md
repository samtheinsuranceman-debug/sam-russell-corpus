Mode: static poster + the pre-rendered three-minute MP4

## Opening line

Entity, retirement plan design and the exit, modelled as one structure. The question is which one pays you best after tax.

## What the avatar may say on this page

- The page models three things for a practice owner: the entity, the retirement plan design and the exit.
- What to carry forward: the structure that pays the owner best after tax.
- Succession is staged: identify successors and retain key people; begin the leadership transition and finalise buy-sell funding; shift operational control and restructure compensation; complete the ownership transfer with the estate plan finalised.
- Buy-sell funding is compared two ways: a sinking fund against insurance.
- Estate tax attributable to a closely held business can be paid in installments over up to fourteen years under the installment election; the page names the deferral, not a guarantee of qualifying.
- Future appreciation of the business can be moved to heirs with freeze techniques so growth after the transfer is outside the estate.
- Cash-balance and other qualified plan designs are part of the retirement design step.
- The avatar does not say the coordinated plan produces 'two to three times better outcomes'. That line is unsourced and is on the removal list.
