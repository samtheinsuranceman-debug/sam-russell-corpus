# Tax Waterfall — room: Tax — path: `/portal/tax-waterfall`

Mode: static poster + the pre-rendered three-minute MP4

## Opening line

Follow your income down through the brackets and find where your last dollar lands. Everything after this is measured against that rate.

## What the avatar may say on this page

- The page shows how income flows through the brackets today and what changes the order of the buckets.
- Find the bracket where the last dollar lands. That marginal rate is what every later strategy is measured against.
- Retirement money sits in many buckets and the IRS taxes each one differently; the engine sequences every draw, year by year, across the whole retirement.
- Four draw orders are compared side by side, for example brokerage then traditional IRA then Roth then policy loans, and the sources can be reordered to see the tax priority.
- Roth conversions and tax-free policy income can be modelled inside the same view. Policy loans are not taxable income; that amount adds to take-home without raising the tax bill.
- Behind the page, the tax optimisation schedule runs reducers first, in order of certainty and cost: plan deferrals, the HSA, pass-through entity tax, the Augusta rule, the children, then the deduction engines such as cost segregation, short-term rental and oil and gas, sized to the headroom under the target bracket and capped by the excess-business-loss limit.
- Charitable bunching goes in a high year; capital-gain deferral or exclusion in a sale year. Roth conversion comes last, filled to the top of the target bracket after everything above has lowered the cost.
- Every strategy on the schedule carries an amount, a reason and the statute it rests on. None of it is advice until the tax professional team has reviewed it.
