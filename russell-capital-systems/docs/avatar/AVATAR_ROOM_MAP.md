appears on the twenty pages below, beside the microphone, with a different

## Live embed or poster

**LIVE** (8 pages): where the visitor is deciding or entering something for
the first time, and a conversation changes what they do next. These are the
pages where LiveAvatar minutes buy something.

- `/` — Every session starts here. The nine screens, the fifteen claims and the lead card. Roughly half of all sessions.
- `/fact-finder` — The page the homepage and the advisor send people to. The second most visited page by design.
- `/ultra-calculator` — The Decade Machine. The flagship public tool and the third destination the advisor offers.
- `/calculators` — The catalogue page: search traffic for 'physician calculator' lands here.
- `/support` — The contact page: booking link, getting started, the strategy engine, team management. The only public page where a visitor can reach a person.
- `/pricing` — The decision page. Low traffic, high intent.
- `/portal/the-arrival` — The first of the seven journey rooms. Every new client passes through it once.
- `/portal/forgiveness` — The physician's largest early-career debt. High emotion, long dwell.

**Poster + MP4** (12 pages): the still of the same face beside the mic, and
the pre-rendered three-minute MP4 on tap. Long-reading and calculator pages
where the visitor works, and a live session would interrupt. The microphone
still answers in six modes on every one of them.

Rule for a new page: live only if a first-time decision happens on it. Everything
else is a poster.
