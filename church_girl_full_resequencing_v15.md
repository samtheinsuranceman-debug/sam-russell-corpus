## RECOMMENDATION: SEQUENCE C

Sequence C remains the strongest option for three reasons:

**1. The opening is the strongest hook in storytelling.** "A woman told me she dreamed our entire life together and I walked past her with plates." She's GRIPPED from sentence one. She HAS to know why he's writing this letter.

**2. The emotional whiplash creates addiction.** She never knows what's coming next — tears, then laughter, then awe, then vulnerability, then adventure. Her brain can't predict the pattern, so it can't disengage. This is the mechanism behind binge-watching.

**3. The spine resolves at position 23.** Three missed women (positions 1, 11, 23) create a pattern that the LETTER itself resolves. She realizes: "I'm the fourth. And this time he's not walking past." That realization is the moment she decides to find him at Mass.

**Exception:** If Bob is definitely reading it first, use Sequence B. The first 8 stories are pure safety — a father reads those and says "This man is safe for my daughter."
