## Elicitation Questions (from pp. 216-219)

1. "When you pick up a book or think about attending a workshop, what do you pay attention to first—the big picture, book cover, or specific details about its value?"
2. "How do you 'run your brain' when you first attempt to understand something new? Do you look first for similarities or differences?"
3. "When you think about something or learn something new, which sensory channel do you prefer?"
4-5. "When you listen to a speech or conversation, do you tend to hear the specific data given or do you intuit what the speaker must mean? Do you want proof and evidence or do you take more interest in your intuitions?"
6. "When you think about things or make decisions, do you tend to operate in black-and-white categories or does your mind go to the steps and stages that lie in between?"
7. "When you look at a problem, do you tend first to consider the worst case scenario or the best?"
8. "As you begin to think about some of your mental constructs... do you find the representations permanent or unstable?"
9. "When you think about the kind of places where you can study or read, can you do this everywhere or do you find that some places seem too noisy?"
10. "When you think about a subject, do you first think about causation, source, and origins (why), or do you think about use, function, direction, destiny (how)?"
11. "When you think about reality, do you tend to think about it as something permanent and solid or as a dance of electrons, fluid, ever-changing?"
12. "When you think about communicating with somebody, what do you tend to give more importance to—what they say or how they say it?"
13. "When you feel threatened, or challenged, by some stress... do you immediately respond, on the emotional level, by wanting to get away from it or to go at it?"
14. "Where do you put most of your attention or reference: on yourself or on others?"
15. "Think about an event in a work situation that once gave you trouble... What experience surrounding work would you say has given you the most pleasure or delight?"
16. "When you come into a new situation, do you usually act quickly after sizing it up or do you do a detailed study of all the consequences before acting?"
17. "What leads you to accept the believability of a thing? Something about it looks right, sounds right, makes sense, feels right to you?"
18. "When you think about a time when you experienced an emotional state, does that bleed over and affect some or all of your other emotional states, or does it stay pretty focused?"
19. "When you think about a situation at work or in your personal affairs that seem risky or involving the public's eye, what thoughts-and-feelings immediately come to mind?"
20. "What do you want in a job (relationship, car, etc.)? What do you want to do with your life?"
21. "Why did you choose your car (job, town, etc.)?"
22. "Do you like to live life spontaneously as the spirit moves you or according to a plan?"
23. "How did you get up this morning? What did you say to yourself just before you got up?"
24. "What would you find as really important in how you choose to spend your next two week vacation?"
25. "Tell me about a goal that you have set and how did you go about making it come true?"
26. "What do you tend to primarily concern yourself with—the price, convenience, time, or quality?"
27. "When you think about having and owning responsibility for something in a work situation or personal relationship, what thoughts and emotions occur to you?"
28. "When you think about meeting someone new, do you immediately have a sense of trust and openness, or thoughts and feelings of distrust, doubt, questions?"
29. "When you feel the need to recharge your batteries, do you prefer to do it alone or with others?"
30. "Do you know what you need in order to feel and function more successfully at work? Do you know what someone else needs?"
31. "How do you typically communicate in terms of placating, blaming, computing, distracting, and leveling?"
32. "When you come into a situation, how do you usually respond? With congruence or incongruence? Cooperation or competition?"
33. "When you come into a situation, do you usually act quickly after sizing it up or do you engage in a detailed study?"
34. "Tell me about a work situation (or environment) in which you felt the happiest, some one-time-event."
35. "How would you evaluate your work as of today? How do you know the quality of your work?"
36. "What source of knowledge do you consider authoritative and most reliable?"
37. "If, in the process of studying something, you had to break off your study and leave it, would this settle well or feel very disconcerting?"
38. "When you think about going out into a social group or out in public, how do you generally handle yourself?"
39. "Evaluate your motives in interacting with others in terms of Power, Affiliation, and Achievement—distribute 100 points."
40. "As you think about this X (a thing, person, event), what do you evaluate as valuable, important, or significant?"
41. "Can someone 'tell' you something? How do you think and feel when you receive 'instructions'?"
42. "Do you think of your value as a person as conditional or unconditional?"
43. "As you think about some of the things that you can do well... make a list. How confident do you feel about your skill?"
44. "How do you experience yourself in terms of your mind, emotions, body, roles?"
45. "When you think about how well or how poorly you live up to your ideals, do you feel integrated or torn?"
46. "Where do you put most of your attention—on the past, present, or future?"
47. "Do you represent 'time' as coming into you and intersected with your body, or outside of yourself?"
48. "Do you represent 'time' as sequential or random?"
49. "When you think about some difficulty arising in everyday life, what usually comes to mind?"
50. "When you think about some misbehavior that you engage in, what thoughts-and-feelings come to you?"
51. "Ask any question that evokes some kind of causational presupposition."
