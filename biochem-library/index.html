Grading Rubric (A–D)

Grades are a transparent, curated tier reflecting an institution's consensus standing across published biochemistry / life-science subject rankings (Times Higher Education, U.S. News Best Global Universities, SCImago Institutions Rankings, Research.com) as of 2025–2026. They are a directory tier, not a precise score, and not our own measurement. Sources are linked on the Method page.

GradeMeaningSignal basis
AGlobal top tierConsistently top-ranked across multiple subject rankings; very high citation impact and output in biochemistry & molecular biology.
BResearch-intensive leaderStrong, sustained output and impact; frequently ranked in the global top 100–200 for the subject.
CSolid national contributorEstablished program with meaningful output; notable regionally or nationally.
DEmerging / specializedGrowing program or narrow specialization; included to track trajectory.

Honesty: a grade is a summary of other people's published rankings, verifiable at the sources on the Method page. Where rankings disagree, the more conservative tier is used. Any institution can be re-graded as newer ranking data is added.

Cross-Pollination Lab — emergent-property workspace

A structured workspace for the real goal: spotting where two established research areas intersect in ways that may be novel and patentable. This is a hypothesis scaffold, not a claim of discovery — each pairing is a prompt to investigate, and every lead must be checked for prior art (start with the live search and a patent database) before it means anything.

Seeded intersection prompts

Prior-art discipline: an intersection returning few results is a lead, not proof of novelty — it can also mean the field uses different terms. Confirm with a patent search (Google Patents, USPTO, Espacenet, WIPO PATENTSCOPE) and a registered patent attorney before treating anything as inventive.

Method & Honesty

What is real here. Every institution is a real institution. Every database and every link resolves to a genuine, public resource. Live search results are fetched from OpenAlex at query time — the titles, authors, years, and DOIs are theirs, not ours. We do not fabricate findings, citations, or DOIs.
What the grades are. A curated tier summarizing published third-party rankings (below), not an original measurement and not a guarantee of any specific study's quality. Individual papers must be judged on their own merits.
What this is not. Not a live scrape of hundreds of university servers, not medical advice, and not a claim that any intersection in the Cross-Pollination Lab is patentable. Patentability requires prior-art search and patent counsel.
Ranking sources. Times Higher Education — Life Sciences 2025 · U.S. News Best Global Universities — Biology & Biochemistry · SCImago Institutions Rankings — Biochemistry, Genetics & Molecular Biology · Research.com — Biology & Biochemistry
How it grows. The institution list is a seed. Add rows to the INSTITUTIONS array (name, city, country, grade, ror). Everything else — filters, live search, cross-pollination — scales automatically. Grades update as newer ranking data is added.
Data backbone. OpenAlex (open, ~250M works, ROR-linked institutions), which itself ingests Crossref, PubMed, ROR, and publisher metadata — the single most efficient way to reach "all universities" without hundreds of brittle connections.
