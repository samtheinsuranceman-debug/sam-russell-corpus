## Key Questions Each Line Answers:
- What am I aware of? (cognition)
- What do I need? (needs)
- Who am I? (self-identity)
- What is important to me? (values)
- How do I feel about this? (emotional intelligence)
- What is the right thing to do? (morals)
- How should we interact? (interpersonal)
- How should I physically do this? (kinesthetic)
- What is of ultimate concern to me? (spirituality)

## Critical Structural Insight

Lines are NOT all independent:
- Cognitive line is a NECESSARY PREREQUISITE for most other lines
- You cannot be at a higher moral stage than your cognitive stage
- Self-related lines (cognitive, moral, emotional, ego) tend to develop together
- Culture determines which lines are prioritized

This means:
- Within-cluster correlations are HIGH (cognitive cluster, emotional cluster)
- Between-cluster correlations are LOW (cognitive ↔ kinesthetic, spiritual ↔ mathematical)
- This validates our assumption of ~4-6 effective independent dimensions from 22 measured lines
