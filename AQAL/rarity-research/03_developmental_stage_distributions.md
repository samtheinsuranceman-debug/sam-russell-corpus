## KEY INSIGHT FOR AQAL RARITY MODEL

The developmental stage models show a CONSISTENT pattern:
- ~60-80% of adults cluster at conventional/middle stages
- ~15-20% reach post-conventional stages
- ~1-5% reach autonomous/integrative stages
- <1% reach the highest stages (unitive/self-transforming)

This maps remarkably well to IQ distribution:
- IQ 100 (average) = 50th percentile = conventional stage
- IQ 115 (1 SD above) = 84th percentile ≈ early post-conventional
- IQ 130 (2 SD above) = 97.7th percentile ≈ autonomous/strategist
- IQ 145 (3 SD above) = 99.87th percentile ≈ construct-aware
- IQ 160+ (4 SD above) = 99.997th percentile ≈ unitive/transcendent

## RARITY COMPOUNDING: Stage + Intelligence Lines

If someone is at Kegan Stage 5 (1% of population) AND has high fluid intelligence (top 2%):
- Independent probability: 0.01 × 0.02 = 0.0002 = 1 in 5,000
- But these are NOT fully independent (correlation ~0.3-0.5)
- Adjusted: roughly 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 3,000

If we add MULTIPLE high intelligence lines:
- Stage 5 (1%) × High Gf (2%) × High interpersonal (5%) × High intrapersonal (3%)
- Independent: 0.01 × 0.02 × 0.05 × 0.03 = 0.0000003 = 1 in 3.3 million
- With correlations (r≈0.3): roughly 1 in 50,000 to 1 in 500,000

This is the mathematical basis for our rarity claims.
