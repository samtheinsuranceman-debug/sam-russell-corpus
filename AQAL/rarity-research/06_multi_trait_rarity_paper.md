# "The Number of Exceptional People: Fewer Than 85 Per 1 Million Across Key Traits"
## Gignac (2024) - Journal of Research in Personality

This is the MOST relevant paper for our model. It directly calculates multi-dimensional rarity.

## Key Findings

### The Three Dimensions Studied:
1. Intelligence (IQ)
2. Conscientiousness (Big Five)
3. Emotional Stability (inverse of Neuroticism)

### Correlations Between Dimensions:
- Intelligence ↔ Conscientiousness: r = -0.03 (essentially uncorrelated)
- Intelligence ↔ Emotional Stability: r = 0.07 (essentially uncorrelated)
- Conscientiousness ↔ Emotional Stability: r = 0.42 (moderately correlated)

### KEY RESULT: Rarity at Different Thresholds (3 dimensions)

| Threshold | Per Dimension | Combined (if independent) | Combined (with correlations) |
|-----------|--------------|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| ≥ Mean (50th %ile) on all 3 | 50% each | 12.5% (1 in 8) | Higher due to positive correlations |
| ≥ 1 SD above mean on all 3 | 15.87% each | 0.4% (1 in 250) | ~0.5-1% |
| ≥ 2 SD above mean on all 3 | 2.28% each | 0.0012% (1 in 83,000) | ~85 per million (1 in 12,000) |
| ≥ 3 SD above mean on all 3 | 0.13% each | 0.0000002% (1 in 500M) | Much rarer than independent calc |

### Critical Insight: Title Says "Fewer Than 85 Per 1 Million"
- Being 2 SD above the mean on ALL THREE dimensions = approximately 1 in 12,000
- This is WITH only 3 dimensions and WITH moderate correlations
- The paper demonstrates that multi-trait exceptionality is MUCH rarer than single-trait

### Exceptionality Categories (from the paper):
- "Notable": ≥ mean on all dimensions
- "Remarkable": ≥ 1 SD above mean on all dimensions  
- "Exceptional": ≥ 2 SD above mean on all dimensions
- "Profoundly Exceptional": ≥ 3 SD above mean on all dimensions

## The Multiplication Rule vs. Reality

The paper confirms:
- Simple multiplication (assuming independence) OVERESTIMATES rarity when traits are positively correlated
- But even WITH correlations, multi-trait exceptionality is still EXTREMELY rare
- The correlation between conscientiousness and emotional stability (r=0.42) means being high on one HELPS you be high on the other
- But intelligence is essentially uncorrelated with both, so it acts as a true independent multiplier

## For Our Model:

- Cross-domain (cognitive ↔ emotional ↔ somatic ↔ spiritual) correlations are LOW (r≈0.0-0.2)
