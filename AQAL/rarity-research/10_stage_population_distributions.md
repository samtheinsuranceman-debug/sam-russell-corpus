# Developmental Stage Population Distributions

## Cook-Greuter Ego Development Theory (EDT) — Stage Distribution

From Cook-Greuter's research across multiple samples:

| Stage | Name | % of Adult Population | Rarity |
|-------|------|----------------------|--------|
| 2 (Pre-conventional) | Impulsive/Opportunist | 5% | 1 in 20 |
| 3 (Conventional) | Conformist/Diplomat | 12% | Common |
| 3/4 | Expert/Self-Conscious | 37% | Most common |
| 4 | Achiever/Conscientious | 30% | Common |
| 4/5 (Post-conventional) | Individualist/Pluralist | 10% | 1 in 10 |
| 5 | Strategist/Autonomous | 4.9% | 1 in 20 |
| 5/6 | Construct-Aware/Alchemist | 0.9% | 1 in 111 |
| 6 | Unitive/Transcendent | 0.2% | 1 in 500 |

**Key finding:** 75-80% of adults are at CONVENTIONAL stages (3, 3/4, 4).
Only 7-17% reach POST-CONVENTIONAL stages (4/5 and above).
Only ~1% reach Construct-Aware or higher.

## Maslow's Hierarchy — Self-Actualization Rarity

Maslow estimated only 1-2% of the population achieves self-actualization.
He identified only ~18 people he considered self-actualized in his lifetime study.

## CRITICAL INSIGHT: Stage × Profile Interaction

The rarity multiplies when you combine:
1. Developmental STAGE (how complex is their meaning-making?)
2. Intelligence PROFILE (how rare is their specific combination of strengths?)
3. Burden CONTEXT (how much adversity are they overcoming?)

Example:
- Stage 5 (Autonomous) = 1 in 20 rarity
- Top 10% on 4 effective independent dimensions = 1 in 10,000
- Combined: 1 in 200,000 (if stage and profile are semi-independent)
- With Tier 3 burden multiplier (1.7x): adjusted to 1 in 340,000

This is how we get to legitimate 1-in-100,000 to 1-in-1,000,000 rarity claims.
