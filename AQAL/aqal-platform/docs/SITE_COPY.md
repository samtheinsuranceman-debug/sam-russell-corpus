## HOMEPAGE — "Maximize. Shield. Connect." (the three pillars)

**Strength Cluster Optimization**
> Your highest lines form **strength clusters** — groups that amplify each other when used together. We
> identify these clusters and show you how to deploy them toward your stated goals. The **second-order
> effects** of combining your top lines optimally are often more powerful than any individual strength.
> Strategic × Interpersonal doesn't just mean "good at planning and people" — it means you can architect
> social outcomes most people can't even see.

**Weakness Cluster Protection**
> Your lowest lines aren't just "areas for improvement" — they're **structural vulnerabilities** that can
> destroy everything your strengths have built. One weak line can collapse the entire system
> geometrically, not additively. We assess the **likelihood** of your weakness clusters threatening the
> system, then prescribe: identify, shield with structural protections, bolster through targeted training
> (permanent gains, per the research), and insure against worst-case scenarios. The evidence shows
> intelligence lines can be trained; the gains persist across decades. Your weaknesses are not destiny —
> but unattended, they control the outcome.

**Complementary Relationship Matching**
> We match you with members whose **strength clusters align with your weakness clusters** — and vice
> versa. The relationship itself is the intervention. Sustained connection with someone strong in your
> weak area produces measurable, permanent improvement in that line — not through formal training, but
> through friendship and shared problem-solving.

## HOMEPAGE — Generational section  *(toggle: SHOW_GENERATIONAL_RARITY, default ON)*

**Eyebrow:** Measured by generation · matched by generation
**Pull quote:** "Rare for your age is the only rare that counts."
> A twenty-five-year-old at the frontier of human development is not a sixty-five-year-old who had forty
> extra years to get there. We score you **against your own generation**, so the young prodigy reads as
> rare — not average — and sheer time-to-compound never inflates the number.

- **Cohort rarity** — Two numbers, both true: how rare you are in the whole population, and how rare you
  are among people your age. The second is the one that moves you.
- **Generational matching** — A generation apart to cover your blind spots (mentor↔protégé); same
  generation to find the peers who move at your pace.

**Closing CTA:** Discover your architecture. Deploy it. → *Begin the assessment — free*

## VOCABULARY (use this everywhere — decided over the week)
- Frame: **directionality · predictability of outcome · protection · efficiency · engineering.**
- Not "how rare and valuable is your brain." Instead: *how **systemized, predictable, protected, and
  engineered** is the aggregate of your mind toward your outcomes, goals, and dreams.*
- **"Engineering outcomes. Re-engineering the mind."** — the least energy, time, effort, and failure for
  the greatest magnitude of desired outcomes.
- Meta-level differentiator: **engineer the strengths, dismantle the weaknesses — after they're identified.**
