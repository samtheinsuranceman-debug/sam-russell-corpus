## MODULE — Humor / Comedy

### What it is
The demonstrated capacity to **reliably change other people's states** — flip a room, a table, a grieving friend, from one emotional register to another, on purpose, repeatably. Not coping humor (self-directed), not jokes-on-command (confounded by performance and taste). The class-clown effect: an observable, repeatable output.

### Why it's independent (and its one honest limit)
- Dissociates from most lines: someone can move a room while being unremarkable at cognition or the g-cluster.
- **Honest limit:** humor-production correlates with *verbal* intelligence (Greengross & Miller; Nusbaum & Silvia), and it re-slices Linguistic + Interpersonal + Influence + Intuitive. So it is **not** one of the independent lines that widen the rarity base — it shows in the profile, graded by stage, but doesn't inflate the 1-in-X. There's a genuine sliver of domain-specific variance (verbally brilliant ≠ funny), which is why it's a real line rather than nothing.

### Elicitation
- **State-change track record.** "Are you the person who shifts the mood in a room? When did that start, and how reliably does it work?" (The class-clown / performer history — observable, not self-flattering trait talk.)
- **Chaos-framing.** "When things fall apart, what do you do with it?" Humor-as-stance shows up as reflexively finding the tragicomic angle under stress.
- **Repeatability.** "Does it land across different crowds, or only with people who already know you?" (Portability = skill, not in-group familiarity.)
- *Caveat:* reading humor off the wild-story answers is confounded with Linguistic — articulate ≠ funny — so keep that a soft signal.

### Nine-stage read
- **Stage 3 (Red).** Humor as dominance and deflection — mockery, cutting others down, controlling the room by force of personality.
- **Stage 4 (Blue).** In-group / conventional humor — bonding through shared norms, safe and role-bound; jokes that police the group.
- **Stage 5 (Orange).** Performance wit — humor as craft and status, the deliberately funny person who works the material.
- **Stage 6 (Green).** Subversive / relativistic — humor that punctures pretension, holds paradox, sees every side.
- **Stage 7+ (integral).** Liberating humor — lightness that frees rather than wounds, holds the tragic and comic at once, used to open rather than dominate.

### Treatment & guardrails
Developmental band + qualitative read. Not numeric, **not fed into rarity** (correlates with verbal g). Before any humor citation hits the research page, verify Greengross & Miller and Nusbaum & Silvia to the standard of the existing eleven.

## MODULE — Parenting

### What it is
The developmental stance a parent brings to raising a child: the balancing act of **warmth, structure, and autonomy-granting**, powered by the capacity to hold the child's inner world in mind. Grades the *parent's stance*, never ranks the child, never scores "good/bad parent."

### Why it's real (and why it isn't in rarity)
- Grounded in **validated** frameworks — Parental Reflective Functioning (Slade 2005; Fonagy et al.; PRFQ, Luyten et al. 2017) and authoritative parenting (Baumrind; Maccoby & Martin; Lamborn/Steinberg/Dornbusch 1991).
- Genuine domain-specific variance: people brilliant with adults can be poor with their own children (different stakes, their own triggers).
- **Not in rarity, and not measured by time-logged.** The evidence is blunt that *quantity* of parental time barely predicts child outcomes (Milkie et al. 2015) — quality and stance do. So it's a formative developmental read, not a scored axis, and definitely not "hours with kids → score."

### Elicitation (reflective-functioning style, not hours)
Model on the Parent Development Interview — ask for **specific recent moments** and probe mental states, the child's and the parent's own.
- "Describe a recent time your child was upset. What do you think was going on underneath the behavior?" (curiosity vs. behavior-only vs. malevolent attribution)
- "A time you clashed — what were you feeling, what do you think they were feeling, and how sure are you?" (owns own experience; holds appropriate *uncertainty* about the child's mind — over-certainty is a failure mode, not a strength)
- "A recent limit they pushed against — how did you decide, and what did you say?" (confrontive/reasoned control vs. coercion vs. caving)
- "A time you got it wrong with them." (reflective humility; rupture-and-repair)

### Nine-stage read
- **Stage 3 (Red).** Child as extension or property; obedience by power; the child's mind not really in view.
- **Stage 4 (Blue).** Duty and obedience; strong structure, warmth conditional on compliance; "because I said so."
- **Stage 5 (Orange).** Optimization — the high-achieving parent engineering a high-achieving child; warmth present but outcome-driven.
- **Stage 6 (Green).** Child-centered, feelings-forward, autonomy-honoring — with the permissive risk of under-structuring.
- **Stage 7+ (integral).** Holds it all at once — warmth *and* firm structure *and* genuine autonomy, tracking the child's own developmental unfolding rather than imposing a template.

### Treatment & guardrails
Formative developmental read, **never a percentile, never into rarity**. No clinical diagnosis (the PRFQ has no clinical cut-offs). Frame as growth, not verdict — a parenting score on one's own kids is emotionally loaded and must be handled as development, not ranking.

## MODULE — Community-Founding / Social Architecture

### What it is
The capacity to **build durable human networks from scratch** — to be the founder and hub who gathers strangers into a circle that holds together for years, and whose defining proof is that **the community outlives the founder's active involvement, and even the founder.** The strongest new line, because its signature output is unmistakable and can't be faked.

### Why it's independent
- **Not Interpersonal.** Interpersonal skill is dyadic and dies with the person. This is *architectural* — it produces a standing structure with its own life. A community that persists after the founder is gone is doing something Interpersonal charm cannot explain.
- Not Influence (persuading one person) and not Community-*membership* (being well-liked). It's origination plus durability.

### Elicitation
- **Origination track record.** "What groups, circles, or communities exist today because you started them? Who was in them at the beginning, and how did they come together?"
- **The durability test (the crux).** "Do any of them still run without you? Would they survive if you disappeared tomorrow?" — The self-sustaining answer is the whole signal.
- **Method.** "When you build a circle, how do you actually do it — how do you turn strangers into a group that sticks?" (reveals whether there's a repeatable architecture or one lucky cluster)
- **Longevity.** "How long have your longest-running circles lasted?" (decades = strong demonstrated floor)

### Nine-stage read
- **Stage 3 (Red).** Gathering a following around one's own power — a crew, a scene centered on the founder's dominance; collapses without them.
- **Stage 4 (Blue).** Building around shared identity, tradition, or institution — clubs, congregations, orders; durable but rule-bound.
- **Stage 5 (Orange).** Strategic network-building — assembling circles for mutual advancement and reach; effective, instrumentally aimed.
- **Stage 6 (Green).** Genuine *communitas* — weaving belonging for its own sake, egalitarian circles that hold because members are truly bonded.
- **Stage 7+ (integral).** Building structures designed to outlast the builder — seeding communities with their own governance and life, so persistence-past-the-founder is intentional, not accidental.

### Treatment & guardrails
Stage band + a **demonstrated floor** from the observable output (how many, how long, self-sustaining or not). This one has a real evidence trail (the communities exist or they don't), so the floor is verifiable. Not a percentile into rarity; the demonstrated floor is the honest anchor.

## MODULE — Financial Self-Management

### What it is
The capacity to **manage money well relative to one's means** — living below income, saving scrupulously, distinguishing want from need and acting on it, avoiding ruinous debt, building net worth over time. Explicitly **self-management**, not market-beating.

### Why it's independent — and why it's the one that can carry a number
- **Not g.** Financial literacy correlates with cognition only modestly, and the *behavioral* piece (actually doing it) dissociates from both intelligence and income — high earners go broke, modest earners quietly build wealth (the *Millionaire Next Door* pattern: net worth predicted by frugality and behavior far more than income).
- **Not just Volitional.** It's domain-specific self-regulation aimed at money; general discipline doesn't guarantee it.
- **The unique feature:** unlike every other new line, this one has **verifiable artifacts** — savings rate, net-worth-to-income ratio, debt behavior. So it can honestly carry a **real numeric measure** alongside the stage read. It's the one place a number is defensible.

### Elicitation
- **Numeric (from artifacts, with consent):** savings rate, net-worth-to-income ratio, debt-to-income, months of runway. These are auditable and objective.
- **Stance:** "What's the difference between something you want and something you need — and give me a recent time you *didn't* buy the want." (want/need discrimination in action)
- "How do you decide what to do with money that comes in?" (default: save, spend, invest, service debt)
- **Market claims are explicitly excluded from scoring.** "I beat the S&P for years" cannot be separated from luck at the population level (survivorship: with enough managers, some win by chance; persistence studies show past outperformance barely predicts future). Frugality leaves an auditable trail; alpha mostly doesn't.

### Nine-stage read (the *relationship to money*, distinct from the number)
- **Stage 3 (Red).** Money as immediate power and gratification — spend it now, get-rich impulses, no horizon.
- **Stage 4 (Blue).** Money as duty and security — save because it's right, thrift as virtue, debt as shame; disciplined but rigid.
- **Stage 5 (Orange).** Money as achievement and leverage — optimizing, investing, building wealth as scorekeeping and freedom-through-success.
- **Stage 6 (Green).** Money as means to values — enough-ness, ethical spending, wealth in service of people and meaning.
- **Stage 7+ (integral).** Stewardship — fluid, non-anxious mastery; money as one instrument among many, held without grasping, deployed with long and wide horizons.

### Treatment & guardrails
**Both** a stage read *and* a numeric measure (the exception among the new lines). The number comes only from real artifacts, never self-reported vibes. Market-beating claims are excluded from the score. Financial data is sensitive — consent-gated, and the numeric measure is optional.
