## Relationships Research Sources (4 pages)
- 11 peer-reviewed sources across 4 themes
- Theme 1: Relationships shape health/lifespan (Holt-Lunstad 2010, 2015; Waldinger/Schulz 2023 Harvard Study)
  - 50% greater survival from strong relationships (on par with quitting smoking)
  - Social isolation independently raises death risk
  - Relationship quality = strongest predictor of long-term health/satisfaction
- Theme 2: Resonance/Similarity draws people together (McPherson 2001; Montoya 2008; Byrne 1961; Block & Grund 2014)
  - Homophily is pervasive across all measured dimensions
  - Perceived similarity robustly predicts attraction
  - Operates across multiple dimensions simultaneously
- Theme 3: Complementarity draws people together (Dryer & Horowitz 1997; Sadler et al. 2010; Estroff & Nowicki 1992)
  - On dominance-submission, complementarity > similarity for satisfaction
  - Partners settle into complementary patterns in real-time
  - Complementary pairings linked to better task performance
- Theme 4: Synthesis (Laursen 2017)
  - Similarity helps friendships FORM; complementary roles help them ENDURE
  - This is exactly the balance the matching engine needs to strike
