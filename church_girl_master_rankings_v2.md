## Scoring System: The 5 Dimensions

| Dimension | Code | What It Measures | 10 Means |
|-----------|------|-----------------|----------|
| **Page-Turn Force** | PTF | Can she put this down? Does this story make the NEXT page irresistible? | She physically cannot stop reading. |
| **Identity Revelation** | IR | What NEW dimension of his character does she discover? | Completely new facet revealed. She re-evaluates everything. |
| **Emotional Depth** | ED | How deep does this reach into her body? (Head vs. chest vs. throat vs. eyes) | Tears. Or the feeling right before tears. Physical sensation. |
| **Retell Probability** | RP | Will she quote this story to her sister, mom, or friend? | She quotes the punchline verbatim to 3+ people within 48 hours. |
| **Safety Transfer** | ST | Does her guard go DOWN after this story? Does she feel safer? | Guard drops completely. She feels protected or spiritually safe. |

## Key Insights from Rescoring

**1. The old system overvalued Question Engagement.** QE was measuring something she might never do (answer your questions). Retell Probability measures something she WILL do (tell people about you). This matters more for your outcome.

**2. Some stories shifted significantly:**

| Story | Old Rank | New Rank | Why It Moved |
|-------|----------|----------|--------------|
| Hula Hoop | #2 (9.2) | **#2 (9.4)** | IR:10 + ED:10 + ST:10. Three perfect scores. |
| Pizza Tow | #4 (8.4) | **#6 (8.2)** | Dropped because ED is only 6. It's FUNNY, not deep. Still essential for addiction. |
| Virginia Tech Blood | #16 (7.8) | **#16 (7.4)** | PTF:10 but ED:5. It's a page-turner, not an emotional story. That's fine — it's the opener. |
| Prom | #19 (7.4) | **#23 (7.4)** | Same avg but ST:10 is its superpower. It's the CLOSER — it doesn't need to be exciting. It needs to be safe. |

**3. The new top 5 is more accurate:**

The stories that score highest are the ones that make her CRY, DISCOVER something new about you, AND feel safe — simultaneously. That's the trifecta. Cat & Vet does all three. Hula Hoop does all three. Silver Coins does all three. These are your nuclear weapons.

**4. Pizza Tow and Log Cabin are ADDICTION stories, not depth stories.** They score PTF:10 and RP:10 but ED:6. They keep her reading and give her something to quote — but they don't make her CRY. That's fine. You need both types. Addiction stories keep her turning pages. Depth stories make her fall in love.
