How this stays honest

Real institutions and repositories; findings, DOIs, and accessions come live from Europe PMC / OpenAlex and are never fabricated. Evidence tiers grade the strength of a study's design, not whether a finding is true. Cross-pollination leads are hypotheses, and draft disclosures are unfiled starting documents — both require a prior-art search and a registered patent attorney before they mean anything. This platform is not a clinical provider, not a data re-distributor, and not medical or legal advice. Your vault, leads, and drafts live in your browser only.
