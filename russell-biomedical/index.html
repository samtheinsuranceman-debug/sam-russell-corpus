The discovery method

A repeatable pipeline from open literature to a counsel-ready lead. Every step is grounded in real data.

Index

Reach the global corpus through open aggregators — ~250M works, ROR-linked institutions — instead of scraping universities one by one.

Grade

Rank sources A–D using a transparent rubric built on published third-party rankings (THE, U.S. News, SCImago, Research.com), so signal is weighted honestly.

Cross-pollinate

Pair established fields and measure how thin the intersection literature is. Sparse, credible intersections become hypotheses.

Stress-test

Run each hypothesis past the multi-AI panel and the live literature to separate real white space from mere terminology gaps.

Prior-art check

Search patent databases before anything is called novel. A thin literature is a lead, never proof.

Draft & refer

Draft a technical disclosure with real module/mechanism detail and hand it to a registered patent attorney, who decides what is filable.
