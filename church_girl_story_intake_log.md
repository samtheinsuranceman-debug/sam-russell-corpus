## Story #28: Homeless Guitarist at Giant

**The Story:** In 11th grade (~16-17 years old), Sam and three friends met a homeless man on the side of Giant grocery store playing guitar. He said he was from California. They were touched by him. All four agreed to sneak him into their parents' houses at night — rotating houses — to let him sleep and eat. They did this for 1-2 weeks. On the second week, while they were all playing pool in the basement, the man said: "I can feel you pushing in my mind. Stop doing that." Then said something else that was really crazy. They decided he might have a mental illness they didn't know about. They dropped him back off at Giant. Good intentions, bad outcome. Lesson learned.

**Punchline (Primary — Option 1):** "We picked him up at Giant. We dropped him back off at Giant. Full circle. Five stars. Would not recommend."

**Punchline (Alternate — Option 2):** "I'd rather have bad judgment with a good heart than the other way around."
