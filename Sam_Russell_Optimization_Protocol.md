Creatine phosphate stores in leg muscles are depleted and resynthesized, increasing creatine utilization efficiency.

**Mechanism:** Nasal breathing forces air through the turbinates, which produce nitric oxide (NO). Paradoxically, breathing "less" delivers MORE oxygen. Additionally, the slower breathing rate (12-15 breaths/min nasal vs. 20-30 mouth) activates the vagus nerve, shifting autonomic balance toward parasympathetic dominance — reducing anxiety and heart rate variability improving.
