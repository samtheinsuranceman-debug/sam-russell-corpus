SE-027

The Signal Box

Critical to fileAlice 7/105-way combination

Built from RCS 001 (Cascading Recalculation) + RCS 013 (Monte Carlo) + Dr. Buddy 09 (Crisis Detection) + JoinAQAL PAT-004 (Weakness) + Drass DR-02 (Compliance Gate)

The simple version

In an old signal box the levers are locked against each other, so a signal cannot clear while the points ahead are wrong. The mechanism makes the unsafe move physically impossible. This invention does that for a financial plan: nothing recalculates and nothing displays until every dependency and every guard agrees. A plan cannot arrive at the screen in a half-changed state. The interlock is in the machine, not the procedure.

How it works

When one figure changes, the system works out everything downstream of it and the order those must move in. Thousands of scenarios are re-run against the new state, and the crisis and weakness signals are checked as part of the same pass. Only when all of it settles, and the compliance gate agrees, does anything appear. A failure anywhere leaves the previous good state on screen rather than a partial new one.

Why it can be patented

Financial tools recalculate optimistically and repair inconsistencies afterward, if at all. The emergent capability is a plan that is never observable in an inconsistent state, achieved by interlocking recalculation, simulation, risk detection and compliance into a single atomic step.

Why this one is critical to file

Atomicity across heterogeneous engines is genuinely hard and genuinely valuable, which is the combination that makes a patent worth owning. It also describes an architecture a competitor cannot easily design around, because the alternative is the inconsistency you are avoiding. Five parents make the claim dense and hard to invalidate piecemeal. This belongs in the very first filing batch.

On the Alice score. Held highest in the set. Atomic multi-engine state transition is a genuine computing problem with a genuine technical solution; this is the least abstract claim here.

DR-02

Refuse-Before-Render Compliance Gate

Critical to fileAlice 6/10Platform patent — Drass Wealth Management

The simple version

Imagine a pharmacist who physically cannot pass you a bottle until every warning is printed on the label. Not a reminder, not a sticky note, a hard stop. That is what this does for financial calculators. A tool that has not satisfied its compliance rules will not draw a single number on the screen. The refusal is part of the machine, not a policy someone can forget.

How it works

Before any figure reaches the page, the tool asks a separate checker whether this particular calculation is allowed to be shown, in this mode, with these assumptions. The checker answers yes or no, and a no produces a plain explanation instead of a result. Historical illustrations carry their own stricter test, because showing what would have happened is the easiest way to mislead someone. Nothing renders around the refusal, so there is no half-answer to misread.

Why it can be patented

Compliance software normally reviews output after it exists, or warns a human. Here the compliance decision happens upstream of generation, so a non-compliant figure is never produced at all. The emergent capability is a calculator that cannot be made to say the wrong thing, even by its own operator.

Why this one is critical to file

Regulated software is heading toward exactly this design, and whoever files first owns the shape of it. The mechanism is visible in the product's behaviour, so any examiner or competitor can observe it working. It also protects the most dangerous surface you have, which is a number shown to a client who then acts on it. Delay here risks both the patent and a compliance incident that a filed claim would have forced you to design around anyway.

On the Alice score. Panel: reads as data gating. Held above the floor because blocking GENERATION differs materially from flagging output, but lowered.

Why it can be patented

Nobody has used psychiatric crisis prediction as the confirming signal for financial behaviour prediction. The emergent capability is a warning that arrives before the irreversible money decision rather than in the statement afterward, with a false-alarm rate low enough that an advisor will actually act on it.

Why this one is critical to file

This is the single most valuable idea in the cross-platform set because it prevents losses rather than reporting them. Both parent mechanisms already exist in code, so the combination is buildable immediately and therefore obvious to a competitor who holds both. On the Alice score. Panel: correlation checking. Held mid because the gating is between two independently trained time-series, not a threshold on one.

Imagine a concert hall that puts on a world-class show every night but does not own one violin. The musicians arrive, play, and leave with their instruments. On the Alice score. Panel: an architectural negative limitation ('cannot run the engines it shows') may add no inventive concept on its own. Lowered.
