Per-seat SaaS subscriptions. Advisors and firms pay monthly per seat for the working
software. Steadiest income; powers the account counts below.
Enterprise licensing. Carriers, broker-dealers, and platforms license an engine to embed in
their own product — the largest checks, the longest sales cycles.
Patent licensing / royalties. Competitors who want the capability license the patent instead
of engineering around 57 interlocking claims.

Channel revenue-share. IMOs, BGAs, and custodians distribute to thousands of advisors
for a share of revenue — reach no direct team can match.
