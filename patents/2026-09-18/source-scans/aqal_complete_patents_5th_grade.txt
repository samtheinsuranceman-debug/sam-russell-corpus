Part I: The Seven Original AQAL Patents

PAT-001: Multi-Model Independent Panel Consensus Engine
The Simple Version: Imagine you have 8 super-smart friends who all take the same test. Each
friend is really good at different things, and they all make different mistakes. But when they all
agree on an answer, you KNOW it’s right! This invention uses 8 AI models to agree on your
assessment, so it’s super accurate.
How It Works: The system sends your answers to 8 different AI models from 8 different labs in
5 different countries. Each model scores your answers separately, without talking to the others.
Then the system combines all 8 scores using a special math formula that cancels out each
model’s mistakes. The result is a score that’s more accurate than any single model could
produce.
Working Components: (1) The 8-Model Panel that sends your answers to 8 AI models, (2) The
Independent Scoring Engine that keeps each model’s score separate, (3) The Error Cancellation
Algorithm that removes mistakes, (4) The Confidence Interval Calculator that shows how sure
the system is, and (5) The Bias Isolation Module that removes unfairness.
Why It’s Patentable: No other assessment system uses 8 independent AI models from 8
different labs to agree on a score. The combination creates an emergent capability: the score is
dramatically more accurate than any single model could produce because the errors cancel out.
This is a brand-new invention that nobody in the industry has done before.

Monetization: 10⁄10 — The foundation of the entire system. Companies will pay a lot for
assessments that are proven to be accurate.



PAT-002: Voice-Based Multi-Dimensional Cognitive Feature Extraction
Engine
The Simple Version: Imagine you could talk to a computer, and it could tell you about yourself
just by listening to your voice. This invention listens to your voice and finds your strengths and
weaknesses. It’s like having a super-smart friend who can hear things in your voice that you
can’t hear yourself.
How It Works: The system records your voice while you answer questions. It analyzes the
words you use (semantic analysis) and the way you say them (acoustic/prosodic analysis). It
looks at things like tone, rhythm, pitch, speed, and pauses. Then it combines all these features
to create a 32-dimensional profile of your cognitive abilities.
Working Components: (1) The Voice Recorder that captures your voice, (2) The Semantic
Analyzer that understands the words you use, (3) The Acoustic Analyzer that understands how
you say them, (4) The Prosodic Feature Extractor that finds tone, rhythm, pitch, speed, and
pauses, and (5) The 32-Dimension Scorer that creates your cognitive profile.
Why It’s Patentable: No other system combines semantic and acoustic/prosodic analysis to
create a 32-dimensional cognitive profile. The combination creates an emergent capability: the
system can find things in your voice that are invisible to text-based analysis because voice
contains information that text doesn’t. This is a brand-new invention that nobody in the industry
has done before.

Monetization: 10⁄10 — Voice-driven diagnostics are more accurate than text-based diagnostics.
Companies will pay a lot for this.



PAT-003: Developmental Stage-Band Reporting Engine
The Simple Version: Imagine you could see not just what your score is, but where you are in
your development. This invention shows you where you are in your development, not just a
score. It’s like having a growth chart for your mind — you can see if you’re getting better or
worse.

stages of growth). Instead of saying “you scored 88,” it says “you’re in the Achiever stage, with

how sure the system is.

stances, (2) The Confidence-Weighted Calculator that shows how sure the system is, (3) The
Range Reporter that shows a range instead of a single number, (4) The Developmental
Trajectory Tracker that shows how you’re changing over time, and (5) The Stage-Band

Why It’s Patentable: No other system reports scores as stage bands with confidence intervals
instead of percentiles. The combination creates an emergent capability: the system can show

nobody in the industry has done before.
