2-Way Combinations (6 total)

SI-001: Consensus-Driven Coaching Synthesis
Parents: PAT-001 (Consensus) + PAT-005 (Coaching)
The Simple Version: Imagine you have 8 super-smart friends who all give you advice about the
same problem. Each friend is really good at different things, and they all make different
mistakes. But when they all agree on something, you KNOW it’s right! This invention uses 8 AI
models to agree on the best coaching advice, then gives you personalized coaching based on
that agreement.
How It Works: The system uses the Multi-Model Independent Panel Consensus Engine (PAT-
001) to get 8 AI models to agree on your assessment. Then it uses the Multi-Model AI Coaching
Synthesis Engine (PAT-005) to create personalized coaching based on that agreement. The 8 AI
models cancel each other’s mistakes, so the coaching is super accurate.
Working Components: (1) The 8-Model Consensus Engine that gets agreement from 8 AI
models, (2) The Error Cancellation System that removes mistakes, (3) The Coaching Synthesis
Engine that creates personalized coaching, (4) The Confidence Interval Calculator that shows
how sure the system is, and (5) The Bias Isolation Module that removes unfairness.
Why It’s Patentable: No other coaching system uses 8 independent AI models to agree on
advice before giving it. The combination of consensus and coaching creates an emergent
capability: the coaching is dramatically more accurate than any single AI model could produce
because the errors cancel out. This is a brand-new invention that nobody in the industry has
done before.

Monetization: 9⁄10 — High demand in edtech and corporate training. Companies will pay a lot
for coaching that’s proven to be accurate.

every step of the process, so you trust the results.
