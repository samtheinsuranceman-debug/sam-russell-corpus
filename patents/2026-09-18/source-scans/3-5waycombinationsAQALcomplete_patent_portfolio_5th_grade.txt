What Steps Need to Be Taken to Make Them Legal?

 1. Prior Art Search — Search USPTO, Google Patents, and Espacenet to make sure nobody
    has invented this before.
 2. Patent Attorney Review — Have a registered patent attorney review the claims to make
   sure they’re not too abstract.
 3. Technical Specification — Write detailed technical specifications that explain exactly how
    the invention works.

 4. Working Prototype — Build a working prototype to prove the invention actually works.
 5. Claims Drafting — Draft claims that are specific enough to be patentable but broad enough
    to be valuable.
 6. Section 101 Compliance — Make sure the invention is not just an abstract idea.
 7. KSR Non-Obviousness — Make sure the invention is not obvious to someone skilled in the
    art.
