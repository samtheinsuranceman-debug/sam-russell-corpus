Allowing every size up to all 78: about 3.0 × 10²³ subsets — more combinations than grains
of sand on Earth. This number is why the strategy is CURATION, not enumeration: the law
only rewards combinations whose members interlock to do something none can do alone.
The value of the raw math is defensive — a competitor cannot search this space either, so a
curated web of filed combinations is practically impossible to design around.
