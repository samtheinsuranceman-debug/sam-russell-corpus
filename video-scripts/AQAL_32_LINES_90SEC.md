Your IQ score feels like it measures your whole mind. It doesn't.

It really only measures about **four** kinds of thinking — and those four collapse into a single factor psychologists call **"g," general intelligence.**

**[beat]**

One number. That's what a lifetime of intelligence gets boiled down to. One.

**[building]**

But your mind is bigger than g. Far bigger.

We measure **thirty-two** distinct lines of intelligence. And **more than sixteen of them are peer-reviewed intelligences that are one hundred percent uncorrelated with g** — real, validated dimensions your IQ score is literally blind to. Logical, spatial, emotional, strategic, interpersonal, intuitive, and dozens more.

**[measured]**

And we don't grade you with one opinion. Every line is scored by a panel of **eight independent AI models**, cross-checked so no single bias decides your result. Each line is measured at least four times. And you don't fill out a bubble sheet — **you talk.** The assessment listens.

**[the payoff]**

What comes back isn't a label. It's a map — your real strengths, and the one line quietly capping all the others.

**[warm, to camera]**

A single number has been hiding most of who you are.

**[beat — close]**

Come see the other thirty-one.
