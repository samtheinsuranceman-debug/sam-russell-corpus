### Key Psychometric Data Found

- **Philosophical (RJI):** High inter-rater reliability; DIT Cronbach alpha ~0.76; longitudinal development through adulthood
- **Tactical (ACS-90):** Cronbach alpha 0.70-0.85 across subscales; predicts task completion independent of IQ
- **Architectural (MicroDYN):** Excellent reliability; predicts school/job performance beyond IQ; CPS loads as separate factor from Gf
- **Parenting (PRFQ):** Cronbach alpha 0.70-0.85; predicts child attachment security and behavioral outcomes
- **Seduction (Flirting Styles Inventory):** Cronbach alpha > 0.80; predicts relationship initiation success
- **Community-Founding (SCI-2):** Overall alpha = 0.94; subscale reliabilities 0.79-0.86

### Prompt Set 1: Strengthen the 6 Contested Lines

**Prompt 1.1 — Philosophical/Epistemic Cognition:**

> Find 3 peer-reviewed meta-analyses or large-scale validation studies for the Reflective Judgment Model (King & Kitchener) or the Defining Issues Test (Rest). I need: (1) evidence that epistemic cognition develops independently of IQ, (2) effect sizes showing growth through adulthood, (3) any factor analysis showing this loads separately from fluid intelligence. Include DOIs for every source.

**Prompt 1.2 — Tactical/Action Regulation:**

> Find peer-reviewed validation studies for Kuhl's Action Control Scale (ACS-90) and Frese's Action Regulation Theory. I need: (1) evidence that action-state orientation predicts task completion independently of conscientiousness and IQ, (2) discriminant validity data showing this is NOT just conscientiousness, (3) any meta-analysis of action regulation interventions. Include DOIs.

**Prompt 1.3 — Architectural/Complex Problem Solving:**

> Find 3 peer-reviewed studies showing that Complex Problem Solving (MicroDYN or similar) is a distinct cognitive factor from fluid intelligence (Gf). I need: (1) factor analysis showing CPS loads separately from Gf, (2) incremental validity of CPS over IQ for predicting job/academic performance, (3) the Greiff et al. 2012 or 2014 validation data. Include DOIs.

**Prompt 1.4 — Parenting/Parental Reflective Functioning:**

> Find the original validation study for the Parental Reflective Functioning Questionnaire (PRFQ, Luyten et al. 2017) and 2 subsequent studies showing it predicts child outcomes. I need: (1) factor structure and reliability data, (2) evidence it predicts child attachment security or behavioral problems, (3) evidence it's trainable (intervention studies). Include DOIs.

**Prompt 1.5 — Seduction/Mating Intelligence:**

> Find the original Geher & Kaufman (2007) Mating Intelligence Scale validation paper and Hall et al. (2010) Flirting Styles Inventory. I need: (1) factor structure showing mating intelligence is distinct from general social intelligence, (2) predictive validity for relationship outcomes, (3) any evolutionary psychology meta-analysis on mate selection cognitive abilities. Include DOIs.

**Prompt 1.6 — Community-Founding/Sense of Community:**

> Find the Chavis, Lee & Acosta (2008) SCI-2 validation study and McMillan & Chavis (1986) original theory paper. I need: (1) the four-factor structure with reliability data, (2) evidence that community-building capacity is trainable or developable, (3) any intervention studies showing people can be taught to build stronger communities. Include DOIs.

### Prompt Set 2: Fill the 11 Therapy-to-Line Gaps

These are AQAL intelligence lines with ZERO therapy coverage. Perplexity should find interventions (not just clinical therapy — also training programs, curricula, deliberate practice protocols) that develop each capacity.

**Prompt 2.1 — Mathematical Line:**

> What peer-reviewed interventions have been shown to improve mathematical reasoning in adults? I need RCTs or meta-analyses of: (1) quantitative reasoning training programs, (2) statistical thinking curricula, (3) mental math training. Not school-age studies — ADULT interventions. Include effect sizes and DOIs.

**Prompt 2.2 — Spatial Line:**

> What peer-reviewed interventions improve spatial reasoning in adults? Find meta-analyses of: (1) mental rotation training, (2) video game spatial training (Tetris studies), (3) 3D modeling or CAD training effects on spatial cognition. Include effect sizes and DOIs.

**Prompt 2.3 — Musical Line:**

> What peer-reviewed evidence shows that music training in adults improves musical intelligence (not just performance skill but auditory processing, rhythm sensitivity, tonal discrimination)? Find: (1) RCTs of adult music instruction, (2) neuroplasticity studies showing structural brain changes from adult music training, (3) transfer effects to other cognitive domains. Include DOIs.

**Prompt 2.4 — Intuitive Line:**

> What peer-reviewed interventions improve intuitive judgment or pattern recognition? Find: (1) Recognition-Primed Decision training studies (Klein), (2) perceptual learning interventions, (3) any RCTs showing deliberate practice improves intuitive accuracy in experts. Include DOIs.

**Prompt 2.5 — Aesthetic Line:**

> What peer-reviewed evidence shows that aesthetic sensitivity or visual literacy can be trained in adults? Find: (1) art appreciation curricula with measured outcomes, (2) design thinking training effects on aesthetic judgment, (3) any psychometric instruments measuring aesthetic sensitivity (e.g., Aesthetic Sensitivity Scale). Include DOIs.

**Prompt 2.6 — Adversarial Line:**

> What peer-reviewed interventions improve competitive/adversarial thinking? Find: (1) negotiation training RCTs with effect sizes, (2) red-team thinking training studies, (3) game theory instruction effects on strategic decision-making, (4) debate training effects on argumentation skill. Include DOIs.

**Prompt 2.7 — Influence Line:**

> What peer-reviewed interventions improve persuasion and social influence capacity? Find: (1) public speaking training RCTs, (2) leadership communication training studies, (3) rhetoric/argumentation training effects, (4) Cialdini-based influence training outcomes. Include DOIs.

**Prompt 2.8 — Financial-Self-Management Line:**

> What peer-reviewed interventions improve financial decision-making in adults? Find: (1) Lusardi & Mitchell financial literacy intervention studies, (2) behavioral finance training RCTs, (3) any meta-analysis of financial education program effectiveness. Include effect sizes and DOIs.
