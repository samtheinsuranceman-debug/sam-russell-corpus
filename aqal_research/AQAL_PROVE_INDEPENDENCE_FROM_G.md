## Why This Matters

If all 32 lines secretly collapse into g + Big Five personality, then:
- The rarity composite is statistically meaningless (you're multiplying correlated probabilities)
- The matching algorithm is matching on noise
- The prescriptions are redundant (everything reduces to "get smarter" or "be more conscientious")
- The whole platform is a fancy IQ test with extra steps

But if even 12-15 lines are genuinely independent axes, then:
- The rarity composite has real statistical power
- The matching algorithm finds genuine complementarity
- The prescriptions target DIFFERENT capacities with DIFFERENT interventions
- The platform measures something no IQ test or personality test captures

## Master Prompts for Perplexity

### Prompt Block A: The G-Factor Question (Most Important)

**Prompt A.1 — How Many Independent Dimensions Exist?**

> According to the Cattell-Horn-Carroll (CHC) model of intelligence, how many BROAD cognitive abilities exist that are genuinely distinct from general intelligence (g)? List each broad ability (Gf, Gc, Gv, Ga, Gs, Glr, Gsm, Gq, Grw, etc.), its definition, and its correlation with g. Which ones have the LOWEST g-loading (most independent)? Include the Carroll (1993) or McGrew (2009) source with DOI.

**Prompt A.2 — Beyond CHC: Non-Cognitive Independent Axes**

> What human capacities have been empirically demonstrated to be UNCORRELATED with general intelligence (g)? I need a comprehensive list of: (1) abilities measured by psychometric tests that show r < 0.20 with IQ, (2) personality traits that predict life outcomes independently of IQ, (3) any "intelligences" proposed in the literature that factor analyses show are genuinely orthogonal to g. Include DOIs for each claim.

**Prompt A.3 — Sternberg's Practical Intelligence (Street Smarts)**

> What is the current empirical status of Sternberg's Practical Intelligence / Successful Intelligence theory? Specifically: (1) Does the Tacit Knowledge Inventory predict job performance AFTER controlling for IQ? (2) What is the correlation between practical intelligence and academic intelligence (g)? (3) Has the construct survived peer review and replication, or was it debunked? Include the key studies and DOIs.

**Prompt A.4 — Gardner's Multiple Intelligences: Empirical Status**

> What is the current empirical status of Howard Gardner's Multiple Intelligences theory? Specifically: (1) Have factor analyses confirmed that his 8 intelligences are genuinely independent? (2) Which of his proposed intelligences have the STRONGEST empirical support for independence from g? (3) Which have been shown to collapse into g? Include Visser et al. (2006) and any subsequent factor-analytic studies. Include DOIs.

**Prompt A.5 — Investment Theory: How G Becomes Domain-Specific**

> Explain Cattell's Investment Theory and its modern extensions. Specifically: (1) How does fluid intelligence (Gf) get "invested" into domain-specific crystallized abilities? (2) Once invested, do these domain abilities become INDEPENDENT of Gf over time? (3) Is there evidence that experts develop domain-specific capacities that are uncorrelated with their general IQ? Include Cattell (1987) and any modern validation. Include DOIs.

**Prompt B.1 — Interoceptive Intelligence vs. IQ:**

**Prompt B.2 — Emotional Intelligence vs. IQ (Ability Model):**

**Prompt B.3 — Self-Control / Volitional Capacity vs. IQ:**

**Prompt B.4 — Mating Intelligence / Seduction as Independent Capacity:**

Find: (1) the factor structure of the Mating Intelligence Scale, (2) whether it correlates with g or is independent, (3) whether it predicts romantic/reproductive outcomes after controlling for attractiveness and IQ, (4) any criticism or replication of the construct. **Prompt B.5 — Financial Literacy vs. IQ:**

> What is the correlation between financial literacy (Lusardi & Mitchell's Big Three questions) and general intelligence? **Prompt B.6 — Resilience vs. IQ:**

**Prompt B.7 — Metacognition vs. IQ:**

**Prompt B.8 — Complex Problem Solving vs. IQ (Architectural Line):**

**Prompt B.9 — Practical/Street Intelligence vs. Academic Intelligence:**

## What You're Looking For in Perplexity's Answers

For each line, you want ONE of these outcomes:

1. **DECLARED INDEPENDENT** — A published researcher formally proposed this as an independent intelligence with a measurement instrument. (Best case.)
2. **EMPIRICALLY INDEPENDENT** — Factor analysis shows it loads on a separate factor from g, with r < 0.30. (Strong case.)
3. **PARTIALLY INDEPENDENT** — Correlates with g at r = 0.30-0.50 but has significant unique variance and predicts outcomes beyond IQ. (Acceptable case.)
4. **G-LOADED BUT DOMAIN-SPECIFIC** — Correlates with g at r > 0.50 but represents invested crystallized ability that becomes functionally independent through expertise. (Theoretical defense.)
5. **NOT INDEPENDENT** — Collapses into g or Big Five. (Honest admission — keep the line but don't claim it's independent in the rarity composite.)
