## Loose End #1: Keystone Practice Line Remapping (19 practices need AQAL-correct lifts)

The keystone practices use non-AQAL categories like "mood," "health," "energy," "memory," "attention," "sleep," "self-regulation," "emotion," "mastery," and "openness." These need to be replaced with actual AQAL line names AND backed by research showing the practice develops THAT specific capacity.

### Prompts:

**Prompt L1.1 — Sleep and Specific AQAL Lines:**

> Find peer-reviewed evidence that sleep deprivation specifically impairs: (1) Volitional capacity (willpower, self-control — ego depletion literature), (2) Strategic thinking (planning, second-order effects), (3) Meta-Cognitive awareness (self-monitoring). I need studies showing sleep loss degrades THESE SPECIFIC capacities, not just "cognitive function" generally. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L1.2 — Aerobic Exercise and Volitional Capacity:**

> Find peer-reviewed evidence that aerobic exercise specifically strengthens Volitional capacity (self-discipline, willpower, follow-through) — not just mood or general cognition. Look for: (1) Oaten & Cheng (2006) exercise and self-regulation, (2) any RCTs showing exercise improves self-control on unrelated tasks, (3) the mechanism by which physical discipline transfers to cognitive discipline. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L1.3 — Mindfulness and Interoceptive Capacity:**

> Find peer-reviewed evidence that mindfulness meditation specifically develops Interoceptive awareness (body signal detection). Look for: (1) studies using heartbeat detection tasks before/after mindfulness training, (2) Farb et al. (2015) or similar showing meditation increases interoceptive accuracy, (3) any meta-analysis of mindfulness effects on body awareness specifically. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L1.4 — Resistance Training and Volitional Capacity:**

> Find peer-reviewed evidence that resistance training (weightlifting) specifically develops Volitional capacity (discipline, follow-through, self-regulation) — beyond just physical strength. Look for: (1) studies showing gym adherence predicts self-control in other domains, (2) any research on the "discipline transfer" from physical training to cognitive self-regulation, (3) military/athletic research on physical training building mental toughness. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L1.5 — Morning Light and Interoceptive/Adaptive Capacity:**

> Find peer-reviewed evidence that circadian anchoring (morning light exposure) specifically improves: (1) Interoceptive regulation (internal body clock awareness), (2) Adaptive capacity (flexibility, adjusting to change). Not just "mood" — the specific cognitive capacities that improve when circadian rhythm is stable. Include DOIs.

## Loose End #3: Axis Modes — Independence Claims

The platform claims 4 lines are "genuinely independent of g" (Adversarial, Interoceptive, Aesthetic, Humor). The audit flagged Humor and Adversarial as questionable.

### Prompts:

**Prompt L3.1 — Humor and General Intelligence:**

Find: (1) Greengross & Miller (2011) or similar studies measuring humor-IQ correlation, (2) any factor analysis showing humor loads separately from g, (3) whether humor STYLE (Martin's HSQ) is independent of IQ even if humor PRODUCTION is correlated. **Prompt L3.2 — Adversarial Thinking and General Intelligence:**

> Is competitive/adversarial thinking (negotiation skill, strategic deception, game theory application) correlated with general intelligence? Find: (1) studies on negotiation skill vs. IQ, (2) Machiavellian intelligence research and its relationship to g, (3) whether game theory performance is predicted by IQ or by a separate strategic capacity. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L3.3 — Interoceptive Accuracy and IQ:**

> Is interoceptive accuracy (heartbeat detection, body signal awareness) correlated with general intelligence? Find: (1) studies measuring both interoception and IQ in the same sample, (2) any evidence that interoceptive accuracy is genuinely independent of cognitive ability, (3) Garfinkel et al. or similar. Include DOIs.

## Loose End #4: Overlapping Lines — Discriminant Validity

22 of 32 lines have overlap concerns. The critical question: can we PROVE these are measuring different things?

### Prompts:

**Prompt L4.1 — Intrapersonal vs. Meta-Cognitive vs. Reflective:**

> These three constructs overlap: Intrapersonal (self-knowledge), Meta-Cognitive (thinking about thinking), and Reflective (examining experience). Find: (1) any factor analysis that separates these three, (2) discriminant validity data between metacognition scales and self-awareness scales, (3) evidence that you can be high on one and low on another. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L4.2 — Strategic vs. Tactical vs. Volitional:**

> These three constructs overlap: Strategic (long-term planning), Tactical (short-term execution), and Volitional (willpower/follow-through). Find: (1) any factor analysis separating planning ability from execution ability from self-control, (2) evidence that someone can be a great planner but poor executor (or vice versa), (3) Kuhl's action-state orientation as distinct from strategic thinking. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L4.3 — Interpersonal vs. Empathic vs. Influence:**

> These three constructs overlap: Interpersonal (social skill), Empathic (feeling-with), and Influence (persuasion). Find: (1) factor analysis separating cognitive empathy from social skill from persuasion ability, (2) evidence that high empathy does NOT predict high influence (or vice versa), (3) discriminant validity between the MSCEIT branches. Include DOIs.

**Prompt L4.4 — Adaptive vs. Resilient:**

> These two constructs overlap: Adaptive (flexibility, pivoting) and Resilient (stress recovery, bouncing back). Find: (1) any factor analysis separating psychological flexibility from resilience, (2) evidence that someone can be highly flexible but NOT resilient (or vice versa), (3) discriminant validity between the AAQ-II (flexibility) and CD-RISC (resilience). Include DOIs.
