## 1. Contextual Baseline: The Boot Camp Mail Reality

To evaluate whether 12 pages is "too much," we must first understand the baseline reality of what an average 19-year-old Army recruit receives during basic training.

**The Typical Boot Camp Letter:**
*   **Frequency:** 1-2 times per week (if lucky). Many recruits receive almost no mail.
