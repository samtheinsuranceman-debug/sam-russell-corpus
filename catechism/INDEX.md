### What Touched Them Most

Across all 28 brothers, the most transformative elements were:

1. **The Real Presence of Christ in the Eucharist** (mentioned in 26/28 summaries)
2. **The Communion of Saints and Interconnection** (mentioned in 25/28 summaries)
3. **The Transformative Power of Grace** (mentioned in 27/28 summaries)
4. **The Pope as Successor of Peter** (mentioned in 24/28 summaries)
5. **The Sacraments as Encounters with Christ** (mentioned in 28/28 summaries)

### Their Transformation

The brothers experienced a consistent pattern of transformation:

- **From intellectual belief to lived encounter**
- **From isolation to communion**
- **From duty as obligation to duty as love**
- **From faith as concept to faith as presence**

All 28 brothers reported:

- Recognition of Christ's living presence
- Understanding of the covenant
- Belief in the transformative power of grace
- Commitment to the Church as the Body of Christ
- Average increase: **+3.1 points (50% increase)**

### Their Increased Duty

- Proclaim the Gospel
- Support the Pope
- Build community and communion
- Transform the world through faith
- Average increase: **+3.3 points (57% increase)**
