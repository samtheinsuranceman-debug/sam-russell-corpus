Method & Honesty

What this produces. A DRAFT technical disclosure in provisional-application format: title, field, background (the two real source findings and their gap), summary of the proposed combination, an enablement scaffold, a claim skeleton, and prior-art positioning. It is a starting document for a patent attorney — not a filed application.
Not reduced to practice. A cross-pollination lead is a hypothesis. The draft says so plainly and lists what would need to be built and experimentally validated before any real filing. Claims are placeholders for counsel to write.
Real sourcing. Background cites the actual findings (with DOIs) from your vault — originally fetched live from Europe PMC / OpenAlex. No fabricated data, results, or citations.
Required next steps (in every draft). Prior-art search (Google Patents, USPTO, Espacenet, WIPO); experimental validation; review and claim drafting by a registered patent attorney. Nothing here is legal or medical advice, and no draft is patent-pending.
Private. Leads and drafts live in your browser only; downloads are generated locally.
