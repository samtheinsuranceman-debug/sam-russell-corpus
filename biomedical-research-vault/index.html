The 12-Tier Evidence Spectrum

Definitive → speculative, grounded in the Oxford CEBM Levels of Evidence and the GRADE / evidence-pyramid tradition. A tier reflects a study's declared design and metadata — how strong the kind of evidence is — not a verdict that the finding is true.

Even Tier 1 can be revised — science is provisional. "Definitive" here means "highest-strength evidence class," not "irrevocable." Tiering is a transparent heuristic from publication type + title/abstract cues; verify any specific paper before relying on it.

Method & Honesty

Real. All 100 institutions are real. Findings are fetched live from Europe PMC / OpenAlex; titles, authors, years, and DOIs are theirs. No fabricated records, findings, or DOIs.
How tiering works. Each finding's evidence tier is assigned from its declared publication type (meta-analysis, RCT, cohort, case report, preprint…) plus title/abstract cues (in vitro, in vivo, in silico, correlation). It grades the strength of the evidence class, transparently — not the truth of the claim.
How "connecting to archives" works. University output — including open-access copies in their institutional repositories — is reached through OpenAlex and Europe PMC, which already federate those archives. No brittle per-library scraping; the coverage is broader and the links are stable.
The vault. Findings you save persist in your browser (localStorage) and export to JSON/CSV — a real, growing, portable database. It lives on your device; it is not uploaded anywhere.
Not. Not medical advice; not a claim any finding is true or clinically actionable; not a re-distributor of controlled data. Cross-pollination for IP requires prior-art search + patent counsel.
