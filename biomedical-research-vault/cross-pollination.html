Method & Honesty

What it does. Combines two real, evidence-tiered findings from different fields into a hypothesis that neither field states alone — the classic route to a non-obvious, potentially patentable invention.
Novelty signal (transparent). score = (evidence strength of A + of B) × (field-distance). Evidence strength is higher for lower tier numbers (T1 strongest). Field-distance is higher when the two findings share few words — a proxy for "these fields rarely meet." It is a PRIORITIZATION heuristic, not a measure of patentability.
Discipline. A lead is a starting point. It must clear a real prior-art search (Google Patents, USPTO, Espacenet, WIPO — links provided) and a registered patent attorney before it means anything. Thin intersection literature can also just mean the fields use different words.
Real & private. Source findings and DOIs come from your vault (originally live from Europe PMC / OpenAlex) — never fabricated. Baskets and saved leads live in your browser only.
Not. Not a claim of invention, not medical advice. Patentability and clinical relevance require separate, proper processes.
